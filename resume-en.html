About

Medical imaging AI engineer who turns qualitative clinical judgment into quantitative, reproducible pipelines across CT, MRI, PET, X-ray, and EEG, and takes models all the way from research to product release and clinical validation. I design and implement full pipelines — segmentation, registration, reconstruction, detection, and GAN-based synthesis — and have scaled them into production systems with end-to-end automated testing, multi-GPU batching, and Docker/CI-CD serving, growing from an individual contributor into an AI team lead.

4 yr 3 mo in medical AI · 3+ shipped products · 4 papers (incl. 1st-author SCI) · 2 registered patents · 2 registered copyrights.
Highlights: lung-cancer RECIST auto-assessment at 91% accuracy (lesion Dice 82%, validated at Asan Medical Center) · PET reconstruction model 257× lighter (SSIM 0.983) · posture-dependent lobar volume change quantified at +90.2 mL (LLL).
